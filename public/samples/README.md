# Sample images for FixMyDelhi

Place three images here. They are loaded by the "Try a sample" buttons on the Home page.

| Filename       | Content                          |
|----------------|----------------------------------|
| `pothole.jpg`  | A clear photo of a road pothole  |
| `garbage.jpg`  | A visible garbage dump on a road |
| `water.jpg`    | Waterlogging / standing water    |

Images must be JPEG or PNG. Any size works — they are resized to 224×224 by the model pipeline.
